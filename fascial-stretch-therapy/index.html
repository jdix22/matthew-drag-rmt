What is fascial stretch therapy?

Fascial stretch therapy (FST) is a gentle, assisted stretching technique. It works on fascia, the connective tissue wrapped around your muscles, tendons, ligaments and joints, which plays a big role in how your body moves and feels.

Repetitive training, sitting all day or an old injury can leave fascia tight and restricted. That shows up as stiffness, pain and a shorter range of motion. FST uses gentle traction and targeted stretches to release those restrictions.

Who it helps

  • Runners and lifters with tight hips, hamstrings or IT bands
  • Hockey and soccer players who feel "locked up" through the hips and lower back
  • Desk workers with stiff necks, shoulders and backs
  • Anyone who wants to move more freely without forcing it

You stay clothed in comfortable athletic wear. Matthew moves you through each stretch while you relax, so you get further than you could stretching on your own.

FST or massage?

Massage is hands-on work on the muscle itself. FST moves whole joints and chains of muscle through their range. Many clients alternate the two, or ask Matthew to combine them. If you're unsure, book whichever suits your time and talk it through at the start of your visit.
