    Questions people ask

    Still wondering? Call or text 905-716-4419.

    What happens at my first massage?

    You fill out a health history form (you can do it online through Jane before you arrive) and talk through what's bothering you and what you're training for. Matthew then steps out so you can get on the table and get comfortable, and knocks before he comes back in.

    Do you direct bill my insurance?

    Yes. Matthew direct bills most major insurers, so you only pay the part your plan doesn't cover. If a claim can't go through, you get an official receipt to submit yourself. Coverage depends on your own plan.

    Which insurance companies do you bill?

    Alberta Blue Cross, Canada Life, Chambers of Commerce Group Insurance Plan, Desjardins Insurance, Manulife, Sun Life, Telus AdjudiCare, and many more. Not sure about yours? Call or text 905-716-4419 and ask.

    Is fascial stretch therapy covered by my benefits?

    Fascial stretch therapy (FST) is billed as massage therapy, so it uses your RMT coverage the same way a massage does.

    Do you come to my home?

    Yes. Home visits are 60, 75 or 90 minutes, from $135. Matthew brings the table, to your home or wherever you train. The price varies with the distance.

    I don't have insurance. Is there a discount?

    Yes. Clients without health insurance can ask about a cash discount on 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 and 120 minute treatments.

    Do you work with teams and events?

    Yes, individuals, competitive athletes and sports teams, plus corporate and community events, paid or pro bono. Send the date and location through the contact form below.

    Do you sell gift certificates?

    Yes. Ask about gift certificates by call, text or email. They make a good "treat yo' self" for the runner in your life.

    What do you recommend for recovery at home?

    Medistik extra-strength cream: loosen tight muscles before activity, then soothe sore muscles and joints after. It's available to buy at the clinic after your session.
