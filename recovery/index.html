What NormaTec does

NormaTec boots (by Hyperice) use dynamic air compression that mimics your muscles' natural pumping action. It's built to speed up recovery, increase circulation and reduce swelling in the legs.

  • Less muscle soreness and stiffness
  • Less swelling and inflammation
  • Better circulation and lymphatic drainage
  • Faster recovery after workouts and races

Hyperice X: hot, cold and contrast

Every NormaTec session on Jane includes the Hyperice X shoulder for contrast (hot and cold) therapy, helpful for a recent injury or a frozen shoulder.

Matthew also has a Hyperice X knee for sore or recently injured knees, and a NormaTec hip attachment for quads, hamstrings, IT bands, lower back and glutes. Ask him about either one.
